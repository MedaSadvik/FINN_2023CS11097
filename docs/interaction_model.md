# FINN interaction model v1

**POV:** FINN is a briefing, not a dashboard. Ranked feed + ask-bar. No voice, no graph, no separate Network tab.

## Screens
1. **Today:** ranked cards. "14 routine filings hidden" row. Window picker 24h / 3d / 7d.
2. **Card sheet:** results numbers, a bio, or event context.
3. **Company:** promised-vs-delivered scorecard, 4 quarters, quote on tap.
4. **Ask:** bottom bar. Answers render as small cards.
5. **Settings:** volume multiple, price-move %, filing types.

## Call Sheet (no score)
Plain facts, each tagged "public profile" or "your notes":
- Domain history: "Semiconductors 2014-2021 (7 yrs). Ops head at Kaynes 2017-19."
- Relation to this company: worked there / competitor / supplier / customer.
- Current reach: "Overlapped with 6 people now working in semiconductors."
- Last spoke: "14 months ago." The investor sets this on the contact's profile (a month picker); it is blank for new connections until set.
- Why now: one line tied to the filing.
- Ask her: two questions.

Candidate order is rule-based: worked at the company, then competitor, then supplier/customer; most recent first.

## Noticed strip
Patterns the investor would miss, e.g. "3 contacts are ex-employees of the company whose CFO resigned."
